输入与本机学习

Rectfly 键盘扩展不联网。中文纠错、英文补全、词语联想和个人学习在设备上完成。输入文字、导入的 Rime 用户词库及学习记录只在本机使用，不上传给我们；只有你在“用 iCloud 同步”下打开“学到的词”后，学到的词才会通过你自己的 iCloud 在设备间同步。“导入通讯录人名”只在你点按时请求通讯录权限，只读取姓名，你确认后加入本机词库，不上传。

App Store 版本在你同意“帮助改进 Rectfly”后发送评测统计；“发送使用统计”默认开启，开启时发送每日使用统计和诊断报告，可随时关闭。这些数据都不含输入文字，也不关联你的身份。TestFlight 版本始终发送评测统计，并且只在你点“发送日志”时发送输入记录。具体见下方说明。App 无第三方 SDK、无广告、无追踪工具。

本机评测记录与 TestFlight 日志

“帮助改进 Rectfly”开启时，键盘在本机保存评测所需的输入记录。记录可能包含输入文字、候选、触点和周边上下文，验证码等普通输入框的内容也可能被记录；iOS 不允许第三方键盘在密码框中使用，因此密码不会被记录。App 中不能查看或导出这些记录。在支持 Apple 智能的 iPhone 上，主 App 还会用 Apple 的本机模型读取这些记录，找出学错的词并降低其排序；这一步也在本机完成。

关闭“帮助改进 Rectfly”会停止采集，但不会删除已有记录；可在“数据”中通过“清除本机评测记录”删除。“清除学习记录”单独重置本机学习。

仅 TestFlight 版本：“数据”中有“发送日志”。点击后，App 把最近 7 天保存的输入记录压缩后通过 iCloud 发送给开发者，内容包括按键、触点、显示的候选和上屏文字。不点击就不发送。App Store 版本没有这个按钮，输入记录不会离开设备。

为什么请求完全访问

Rectfly 键盘扩展声明请求“允许完全访问”。它用于键盘与主 App 在本机共享设置、按键震动级别、用户词库、个人学习数据，以及主 App 安装的完整词库和语法模型，也用于共享本机评测记录，以及在键盘显示时读取普通剪贴板文字并在本机保存剪贴板历史。

完全访问会扩大 iOS 授予键盘的权限，包括联网能力；Rectfly 键盘没有联网代码，不使用该能力。基础输入可不授予完全访问，但本机共享与相关功能会受限。你可随时在 iOS 设置中关闭它。查看操作方法。

剪贴板历史

快速粘贴和剪贴板历史默认关闭，需要你在键盘里确认开启或在主 App 设置中打开；两项都关闭时键盘不读取剪贴板。开启并授予完全访问后，键盘显示期间检测到新的复制时会读取普通剪贴板文字，系统可能显示粘贴提示。历史仅存于本机 App Group，不上传、不同步，并排除系统备份。最多保存 50 条，保存时长可选 10 分钟、1 小时、7 天（默认）或 3 个月；下次读取时清理过期项。你可以单条删除、全部清空，关闭历史时已保存的内容会被删除。

带 Concealed / Transient 标记的内容不会收录；iOS 不公开来源过期时间，因此不能保证识别所有密码或临时文字。空白或超过 16 KiB 的文字也不会收录。快速粘贴提示在首次检测到新副本后三分钟内显示，使用、关闭或开始输入后收起；历史支持整段粘贴或滑动选词。

iCloud 同步

在“数据”中打开“用 iCloud 同步”后，主 App 通过你的 iCloud 账户同步键盘设置，例如拼音方案、语言、自动纠错、按键震动、键盘尺寸、主题、强调色和自定义短语，让同一 Apple 账户下的设备使用相同设置。这些数据保存在你自己的 iCloud 键值存储中，我们无法访问。输入记录、剪贴板和导入的用户词库不参与同步。自定义键盘图标的照片只在本机裁切成小图保存，不上传、不同步。“用 iCloud 同步”在新设备上默认关闭；更新前已在用 iCloud 同步的设备保持开启。打开后其下的“设置”默认开启，你可以单独关闭它，或关闭“用 iCloud 同步”。

学到的词默认不同步，需要你在“用 iCloud 同步”下打开“学到的词”并确认。打开后，主 App 在前台时把键盘学到的词（词语、对应按键和使用次数，以及你删除过的词和清除学习记录的时间，以免它们在其他设备上恢复）保存到你自己 iCloud 账户的 CloudKit 私有数据库，并与同一 Apple 账户下的其他设备合并。私有数据库只有你能访问，我们无法读取。键盘本身不联网，只读取主 App 写入本机的结果。关闭“学到的词”或“用 iCloud 同步”后同步立即停止；关闭“学到的词”时 App 会询问是否同时删除 iCloud 中的副本。本机学到的词保留。

键盘离线，主 App 只连接 Apple

键盘扩展的输入过程不使用网络。完整词库、词包、英文模型和万象语法模型由 Apple 托管，iOS 在安装或更新 App 后自动从 Apple 下载，主 App 校验后安装到键盘使用的本机存储。键盘的翻译使用 Apple 的本机翻译模型，翻译语言由 iOS 从 Apple 下载。

主 App 发送评测统计、使用统计、诊断报告和（仅 TestFlight 版本）日志时，使用 Apple 的 CloudKit 公共数据库（容器 iCloud.sh.lyc.rectfly），需要登录 iCloud；同步学到的词使用同一容器中你自己的私有数据库。除此之外，App 不连接 GitHub、Cloudflare 或其他服务器。“关于”中的外部链接只在你点击时用浏览器打开。Apple 会接收建立连接所需的 IP 地址等信息，并按其隐私政策处理。

联邦评测

联邦评测使用本机输入记录，将最多三个试验组分别与当前方案对比，计算在本机完成，不改变当前键盘。统计衡量每种方案下你需要多做的操作次数（点选候选、展开面板或重打），不代表输入正确率。

App Store 版本在设置向导的“本机评测”一步询问，这一步写明“在本机对比，满 500 次自动发送打字统计。不含你打的字。”从未回答过的人，这一步默认为“关”。离开这一步时按你的选择生效：选“开”即开启本机评测，并同意满 500 次对比后在 App 前台自动发送统计；选“关”则评测和自动发送都保持关闭。没有到这一步就结束设置时，主页会弹出一次“帮助改进 Rectfly？”，说明相同，选“不用了”保持关闭。在设置向导中关闭自动纠错时不会询问，评测保持关闭。选择前不采集、不发送。之后可在“本机评测”页开关“帮助改进 Rectfly”，打开时会再次询问，打开即表示同意自动发送。TestFlight 版本始终开启评测和自动发送，开关不可关闭。

评测结果包括以下汇总计数：每种方案的词数、额外操作次数、过度纠正的次数及其按键数，按错误类型、方案类别、音节数和是否为学到的词分组，已上屏的词和删掉重打的词分开统计；每个试验组与当前方案逐条比较的差值汇总，以及只有一方把目标词排在首位的次数；每个键周围触点落点的计数（按偏移位置分格）和实际落到哪个键的计数，按机型、键盘宽高和横竖屏分组；每个键的触点大小、按住时长和两次按键间隔的计数，按打字快慢分组；单手或双手打字的估计；各类按错被纠正的次数和所选候选的位置；漏按、多按、按反、按到相邻键和只按声母的次数，以及总按键数和音节数；离按键太远、未计入落点分格的触点数；刚上屏的词被删掉末尾的次数；键盘高度设置；以及实验标识、构建版本标识、完成日期和随机提交编号。提交编号只用于防止重复计数。

逐词记录：评测结果还附有最近最多 3,000 个词的打字记录，顺序已打乱。每个词一份，包含语言、输入方案和键盘层；词的长度：字数、字母数或音节数、零声母音节，英文另含空格位置；每次按键对应哪个音节或字母，是否多按、按反或按成别的键，是否按到相邻键，与上一次按键的大致间隔（以 80、150、300 毫秒为界分四档），以及漏按的位置；怎样上屏、选了第几个候选、是否只上屏一部分或放弃、是否被自动纠正、候选中是否有原样输入，以及上屏前的停顿（以 100 毫秒为单位）；随后 3 秒内是否删除或撤销，3 秒或 30 秒内是否重打；大写字母的位置和是否用了 Shift；你按的键是否拼出一个词；以及首选候选的每个音节与你的按键怎样对应。两个字以上且按了四个键以上的词，另附前五个候选的相对信息：显示位置和重排前的位置、是否被选中、字数和音节数、每个音节与按键的匹配情况、与首选的分数差和词频差（粗略分档）、纠错代价和学习加成（按 0.5 取整）、是否从你这里学到及其来源。逐词记录不含按键本身、输入文字、拼音、候选词、绝对分数或词频，也不含触点位置、触点大小和按住时长。

按键落点：评测结果还附有最多 12,000 次按键的落点，顺序已打乱，与逐词记录分开存放，两者之间没有关联；只取自最多打错一处的词。每次按键包含想按的键、落在键上的位置（相对键中心，以键宽和键高的 2% 为一格）、触点半径（2 pt 一档）、按住时长（20 毫秒一档）、与上一次按键的大致间隔、键盘布局和设备分组，以及上一个键是同一个键、同一只手还是另一只手，和它在左侧、右侧还是上下方向。设备分组为机型、键盘宽高（以 10 pt 为单位）和横竖屏。逐词记录和按键落点附带构建版本、资源包版本和实验标识。

随机编号：每次上传评测结果时，App 新生成一个随机编号，只用来把这次上传中的记录归在一起。这个编号不保存在设备上，下次上传会换新，所以不同的上传无法据此关联起来。新词报告、打字摘要、使用统计和诊断报告不带这个编号。评测结果不含输入文字、所选词语、拼音或按键顺序。你可以在“了解本机评测”或评测结果中打开“查看上传内容”。

新词：开启“帮助改进 Rectfly”时（TestFlight 版本始终开启），App 每天最多发送两份报告，内容是你的 iPhone 新学会、但不在 Rectfly 词库中的词，每个词最多报告一次。看起来像人名的词，以及数字、代码、链接、邮箱、电话号码和地址，从不发送。每份报告都在你的 iPhone 上加了本地差分隐私保护（ε = 4）：词被哈希到 1,024 位，发送前约八分之一的位被随机翻转，所以单份报告无法还原出词。报告不含设备标识、日期或任何能把报告关联到你或彼此关联的编号。CloudKit 会为每条记录附上保存时间、一个仅限本 App 的 iCloud 账户标识和设备标识；开发者的工具下载记录时丢弃这两个标识，从不使用。我们只能从所有报告的汇总中得知很多人都在打的词。

打字摘要：开启“帮助改进 Rectfly”时（TestFlight 版本始终开启），App 大约每周在前台发送一份打字摘要，中文和英文分开统计，只含计数：按键次数；上屏的词数及其来自空格、候选栏或展开面板的次数；所选候选位置的分布，以及排序改对、改错和选中学到的词的次数；被自动纠正的次数及其中被撤回的次数；刚上屏就被删除或删除后重打的次数；展开面板的次数；键盘计算候选所用时长的分段计数；长按删除、剪贴板、键盘尺寸和长按符号的使用次数；以及语法是否已安装、完全访问是否开启。附构建版本标识、所在周和随机记录编号，不含输入文字、词语、拼音、按键顺序、机型或比一周更细的时间。某种语言一周不足 200 个词时不发送这种语言的统计。

TestFlight 版本每天还会发送一份运行报告，只含计数和原因代码（后台评测是否被安排、运行和完成，运行时长，内存峰值，以及后台 App 刷新、低电量模式和语法是否开启），记录名为随机 ID，不含任何文字或设备标识。

统计保存在 Rectfly 的 CloudKit 公共数据库，只有开发者能读取。CloudKit 会给每条记录附上保存时间、一个仅限本 App 的 iCloud 账户标识和保存记录的设备标识。这些标识不透露你的 Apple ID；开发者的工具下载记录时会将其丢弃，从不使用，所以开发者无法把记录对应到你，也无法把不同的记录关联起来。每个实验只发送一份评测结果，打字摘要每周最多一份；发送失败时 App 会保留结果，在前台重试。关闭“帮助改进 Rectfly”会取消待发送的结果并清除评测结果文件，原始输入记录需单独清除；已经接收的统计不会撤回。统计不用于广告或跨应用追踪，保留至开发者删除。如需删除，请联系 rectfly@fastmail.com；由于结果不关联身份，我们可能需要相应的提交编号才能定位记录。

使用统计

“本机评测”页有“发送使用统计”开关，所有版本默认开启，首次使用时不会询问；它与“帮助改进 Rectfly”相互独立。开启时，键盘、主 App 和共享扩展在本机按天累计计数，主 App 在前台时为每个已过去至少两天的日子向同一个 CloudKit 公共数据库发送一条记录，记录名每天随机生成。

每条记录包含：日期、构建版本标识和是否为 TestFlight 版本；当天键盘打开和上屏的次数；约 90 项功能各自的使用次数，例如输入方案、滑行输入、展开面板、剪贴板、翻译、表情、撤销纠正、导入词库、主题和同步；发送时的设置，只含开关、固定选项、主题类型，以及键盘尺寸、位置、剪贴板保存时长和震动强度的粗略分档；以及键盘运行状况：中文和英文分开的候选显示延迟分档计数（以 16、33、50、100、250 毫秒为界）、键盘意外终止的次数、各模型加载失败的次数和键盘内存峰值的分档。记录不含输入文字、词语、拼音、按键、其他 App 的标识或比一天更细的时间。CloudKit 附上的 iCloud 账户标识和设备标识在下载时丢弃，从不使用，所以不同日子的记录无法对应到同一台设备或你。

关闭“发送使用统计”会立即停止计数，并删除本机尚未发送的计数和诊断报告；再次开启后从零开始。本机计数最多保留 40 天。已经接收的记录不会撤回，保存在 Rectfly 的 CloudKit 公共数据库，只有开发者能读取，保留至开发者删除，不关联身份，不用于广告或跨应用追踪。

崩溃与性能报告

主 App 和键盘通过 Apple 的 MetricKit 收集诊断报告，包括崩溃、卡顿、CPU 用量超限和磁盘写入超限。每份报告含异常类型和代码、调用栈（只保留二进制文件名、UUID 和偏移位置），以及 App 版本和 iOS 版本。报告不含输入文字。主 App 在前台时把每份报告上传到同一个 CloudKit 公共数据库，用于修复卡顿和崩溃。

诊断报告跟随“发送使用统计”开关：开关默认开启，开启后才收集；关闭后停止收集并删除尚未发送的报告。CloudKit 附上的 iCloud 账户标识和设备标识在下载时丢弃，从不使用。报告不关联身份，不用于追踪。

本网站与联系

本网站使用静态 HTML、CSS 和本地图片，首页另有一个本地脚本用于动画；无 Cookie、无追踪、无第三方嵌入。首页从 Google Fonts 加载字体，Google 会收到你的 IP 地址等请求信息。外部来源链接仅在你点击时打开。

网站由 Cloudflare 和 GitHub Pages 托管，访问时托管服务可能处理 IP 地址等必要请求信息。网站不收集你的键盘输入。

隐私问题或数据处理咨询，请联系 rectfly@fastmail.com。你主动发送的邮件内容会交由邮件服务处理，请避免附带敏感输入记录。

Privacy policy · English

Updated November 24, 2026

Typing and local learning

The Rectfly keyboard extension does not access the network. Correction, completion, suggestions and personal learning run on your device. Typed text, imported Rime user dictionaries and learning records stay local and are never uploaded to us. Learned words sync between your devices through your own iCloud only if you turn on Learned Words under Sync with iCloud. Import Contact Names asks for Contacts access only when you tap it, reads names only, and adds them to the local dictionary after you confirm; they are not uploaded. The App Store version sends evaluation statistics after you agree to Help Improve Rectfly. Send Usage Statistics is on by default and, while on, sends daily usage statistics and diagnostic reports; you can turn it off at any time. None of this contains typed text or is linked to your identity. TestFlight builds always send evaluation statistics, and send input records only when you tap Send Logs. Details follow. There are no third-party SDKs, ads or tracking tools.

Local evaluation records and TestFlight logs

While Help Improve Rectfly is on, the keyboard stores the input records evaluation needs on your device. Records may contain text, candidates, touch events and surrounding context, including verification codes entered into ordinary fields. iOS does not allow third-party keyboards in password fields, so passwords are never recorded. The app cannot show or export these records. On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, the app also reads these records with Apple's on-device model to find words it learned by mistake and rank them lower; this also happens on your device.

Turning Help Improve Rectfly off stops collection but does not delete existing records. Use Clear Evaluation Records under Data to delete them. Clear Learning Data resets local learning separately.

TestFlight builds only: Data has a Send Logs button. When you tap it, the app compresses the input records kept from the last 7 days and sends them to the developer through iCloud. They include keys, touches, shown candidates and committed text. Nothing is sent unless you tap it. The App Store version has no such button, and input records never leave the device.

Full Access

The extension requests Full Access to share settings, haptic preferences, imported dictionaries, personal learning, and the full lexicon and grammar model installed by the app between the app and keyboard on your device, as well as local evaluation records. It also permits reading ordinary clipboard text when the keyboard appears and storing clipboard history locally. iOS also grants network capability with this permission; Rectfly's keyboard contains no networking code and does not use it. Basic typing works without Full Access, while shared features are limited. You may revoke it in iOS Settings at any time.

Clipboard history

Quick paste and clipboard history are off by default; you turn them on from the keyboard's prompt or in the app's settings. With both off, the keyboard does not read the clipboard. When enabled with Full Access, ordinary clipboard text is read when a new copy is detected while the keyboard is shown, and the system may display a paste notification. History stays in the local App Group, without upload or sync, and is excluded from system backups. Up to 50 entries are kept for 10 minutes, 1 hour, 7 days (default) or 3 months, as you choose; expired entries are removed on the next read. You can delete entries individually or clear all, and turning history off deletes what was kept.

Content marked Concealed or Transient is skipped. iOS does not expose the source expiration date, so not all passwords or temporary text can be identified. Blank text and text exceeding 16 KiB are also skipped. The quick-paste offer lasts up to three minutes after a new copy is first observed, or until dismissed, used or typing begins. History supports pasting whole entries or selecting words by sliding.

iCloud sync

When you turn on Sync with iCloud under Data, the app syncs keyboard settings, such as the pinyin layout, languages, auto-correction, haptics, keyboard size, themes, accent color and custom phrases, through your iCloud account so devices on the same Apple Account share them. They are kept in your own iCloud key-value storage; we cannot access them. Input records, clipboard and imported dictionaries are not synced. A custom keyboard logo photo is cropped to a small image and kept on the device only; it is not uploaded or synced. Sync with iCloud is off on a new device; a device that was already syncing with iCloud before the update keeps it on. Once it is on, Settings under it starts on; you can turn Settings off on its own, or turn off Sync with iCloud.

Learned words do not sync by default; you turn on Learned Words under Sync with iCloud and confirm. When it is on, the app, while in the foreground, saves the words the keyboard has learned (each word, its keys and use count, plus the words you removed and when you cleared learning data, so they do not come back on another device) to the CloudKit private database in your own iCloud account and merges them with your other devices on the same Apple Account. Only you can access your private database; we cannot read it. The keyboard itself does not use the network and only reads what the app writes on the device. Turning off Learned Words or Sync with iCloud stops sync at once; when you turn off Learned Words, the app asks whether to also delete the copy in iCloud. Learned words on the device are kept.

Network

The keyboard extension does not use the network. The full lexicon, word packs, English model and Wanxiang grammar model are hosted by Apple; iOS downloads them from Apple after the app is installed or updated, and the app verifies and installs them into the local storage the keyboard reads. The keyboard translates with Apple's on-device translation models; iOS downloads the translation languages from Apple. To send evaluation statistics, usage statistics, diagnostic reports and (TestFlight only) logs, the app uses Apple's CloudKit public database (container iCloud.sh.lyc.rectfly), which needs an iCloud sign-in. Learned word sync uses your own private database in the same container. The app contacts no other servers, including GitHub or Cloudflare. Links under About open in your browser only when you tap them. Apple receives connection information such as your IP address and handles it under its privacy policy.

Federated evaluation

Evaluation uses local input records to compare up to three trial groups individually against the current configuration. It runs on your device and does not change the current keyboard. Counts measure how many extra actions (tapping a candidate, opening the panel or retyping) each configuration would have needed, not typing accuracy.

The App Store version asks in the On-Device Evaluation step of the setup guide, which says “Rectfly compares on this iPhone and sends typing statistics automatically after 500 comparisons. Never what you type.” If you have never answered, the step starts at Off. Your choice applies when you leave the step: On turns on on-device evaluation and consents to automatic uploads after 500 comparisons while the app is in the foreground; Off leaves evaluation and automatic sending off. If you close setup before that step, the app's home page asks “Help Improve Rectfly?” once with the same sentence, and No Thanks leaves it off. If you turn auto-correction off during setup, you are not asked and evaluation stays off. Nothing is collected or sent before you choose. You can later switch Help Improve Rectfly on the On-Device Evaluation page; turning it on asks again and consents to automatic sending. TestFlight builds always have evaluation and automatic sending on, and the switch cannot be turned off.

The evaluation result includes these aggregate counts: for each configuration, totals of words, extra actions, and overcorrections with their key counts, grouped by error type, scheme group, syllable count and whether the word was one it had learned, with committed and retyped words counted apart; for each trial, summed per-record differences from the current configuration and how often only one of the two put the intended word first; for each key, counts of where touches landed around it (in offset bins) and which key they registered on, grouped by device model, keyboard width and height, and orientation; per-key counts of touch contact size, press length and time between presses, grouped by typing speed; an estimate of one-handed versus two-thumb typing; how often each kind of typing error was corrected and which candidate position was picked; counts of missed, extra, swapped, neighbouring and initial-only keys, with total key presses and syllables; the number of touches too far from a key to fall in the landing grid; how often a just-committed word was trimmed; the keyboard height setting; experiment and build identifiers; a completion date; and a random submission ID. The submission ID only prevents double counting.

Word records. The result also carries a typing record for each of your most recent words, up to 3,000, in shuffled order. Each record holds the language, input scheme and keyboard layer; the word's length: characters, letters or syllables, syllables without an initial, and for English where the spaces were; for each tap, which syllable or letter it was for, whether it was extra, swapped or hit another key, whether it hit a neighbouring key, and the rough gap since the previous tap (four bands split at 80, 150 and 300 ms), plus where taps were missed; how the word was committed, which candidate position you picked, whether you committed only part of it or abandoned it, whether autocorrect changed it, whether your literal input was offered, and the pause before committing (in 100 ms steps); whether you deleted or undid it within 3 seconds, or retyped it within 3 or 30 seconds; which letters were capitals and whether you used Shift; whether the keys you typed spell a word; and how each syllable of the top candidate lined up with your taps. For words of two or more characters typed with four or more keys, it adds relative details of the top five candidates: shown position and position before reranking, whether it was picked, character and syllable counts, how each syllable matched your taps, the score and frequency gaps to the top candidate (in coarse steps), typo cost and learning boost (rounded to 0.5), whether it was learned from you, and its sources. Word records contain no keys, typed text, pinyin, candidate words, absolute scores or frequencies, and no touch positions, touch sizes or hold times.

Tap positions. The result also carries up to 12,000 taps in shuffled order, stored apart from the word records with no link between the two, and taken only from words with at most one slip. Each tap holds the key you meant, where on it you landed (from the key's center, in steps of 2% of the key's width and height), the touch radius (in 2 pt steps), the hold time (in 20 ms steps), the rough gap since the previous tap, the keyboard layout and device group, and whether the previous key was the same key, on the same hand or on the other hand, and whether it sat to the left, to the right or above or below. A device group is the device model, keyboard width and height (in 10 pt steps) and orientation. Word records and tap positions carry the build, asset pack revision and experiment identifiers.

Random ID. For every upload of the evaluation result, the app generates a new random ID that only groups the records inside that upload. The ID is not stored on the device and the next upload gets a new one, so separate uploads cannot be linked by it. New-word reports, typing summaries, usage statistics and diagnostic reports do not carry this ID. The evaluation result contains no typed text, selected words, pinyin or key order. Open View Upload Contents under About On-Device Evaluation or in your results to see it.

New words. While Help Improve Rectfly is on (always on in TestFlight builds), the app sends at most two reports a day about words your iPhone newly learned that are not in Rectfly's dictionaries, and reports each word at most once. Words that look like personal names, and numbers, codes, links, email addresses, phone numbers and addresses, are never included. Each report is protected on your iPhone with local differential privacy (ε = 4): the word is hashed into 1,024 bits and about one bit in eight is flipped at random before sending, so a single report cannot reveal the word. Reports carry no device ID, date or other number that links them to you or to each other. CloudKit adds the save time, an app-specific iCloud account identifier and a device identifier to every record; the developer's tools drop both identifiers on download and never use them. We can only learn words that many people type, from all reports combined.

Typing summary. While Help Improve Rectfly is on (always on in TestFlight builds), the app sends a typing summary about once a week while it is in the foreground, with Chinese and English counted apart and counts only: key presses; words committed and how many came from the space bar, the candidate bar or the expanded panel; which candidate positions were picked, and how often ranking fixed or broke a pick or a learned word was picked; how often a word was autocorrected and how often that was undone; how often a just-committed word was deleted, or deleted and retyped; panel opens; binned counts of how long the keyboard took to show candidates; how often held delete, the clipboard, the size panel and long-press symbols were used; and whether the grammar was installed and Full Access was on. It carries a build identifier, the week and a random record ID, and no typed text, words, pinyin, key order, device model or time finer than the week. A language with fewer than 200 words that week is left out.

TestFlight builds also send a daily run report with counts and reason codes only (whether background evaluation was scheduled, ran and finished, run lengths, peak memory, and whether Background App Refresh, Low Power Mode and the grammar were on), under a random record ID, with no text or device identifiers.

Statistics are saved to Rectfly's CloudKit public database, readable only by the developer. CloudKit adds the save time, an app-specific iCloud account identifier and an identifier of the device that saved it to every record. These identifiers do not reveal your Apple ID; the developer's tools drop them on download and never use them, so the developer cannot tie records to you or to each other. One evaluation result is sent per experiment and at most one typing summary a week; failed uploads are kept and retried while the app is in the foreground. Turning evaluation off cancels pending uploads and clears result files; raw input records must be deleted separately. Statistics already received are not recalled. They are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking and remain stored until the developer deletes them. Contact rectfly@fastmail.com for deletion requests. Because results are not linked to identities, we may need the relevant submission ID to locate a record.

Usage statistics

The On-Device Evaluation page has a Send Usage Statistics switch. It is on by default in every build, and the app does not ask on first use; it works independently of Help Improve Rectfly. While it is on, the keyboard, the app and the share extension count on the device per day, and the app, while in the foreground, sends one record for each day that ended at least two days ago to the same CloudKit public database, under a record name that is random for each day.

Each record contains: the date, a build identifier and whether the build is a TestFlight build; how many times the keyboard opened and committed text that day; how often each of about 90 features was used, such as input schemes, glide typing, the expanded panel, the clipboard, translation, emoji, undoing a correction, dictionary import, themes and sync; the settings at upload time, limited to switches, fixed choices, theme kinds, and coarse bands for keyboard size and position, clipboard retention and haptic strength; and keyboard health: binned counts of how long candidates took to show, Chinese and English apart (bands split at 16, 33, 50, 100 and 250 ms), how often the keyboard ended unexpectedly, how often each model failed to load, and a band for the keyboard's peak memory. Records contain no typed text, words, pinyin, keys, identifiers of other apps or time finer than the day. The iCloud account and device identifiers that CloudKit adds are dropped on download and never used, so records from different days cannot be tied to one device or to you.

Turning Send Usage Statistics off stops counting at once and deletes unsent counts and diagnostic reports on the device; turning it back on starts again from zero. Counts are kept on the device for at most 40 days. Records already received are not recalled; they are kept in Rectfly's CloudKit public database, readable only by the developer, until the developer deletes them. They are not linked to your identity or used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Crash and performance reports

The app and keyboard collect Apple MetricKit diagnostic reports for crashes, hangs, excessive CPU use and excessive disk writes. Each report holds the exception type and code, call stacks (binary names, UUIDs and offsets only), and app and iOS versions. Reports contain no typed text. While in the foreground, the app uploads each report to the same CloudKit public database to fix hangs and crashes. Diagnostic reports follow the Send Usage Statistics switch: the switch is on by default, and reports are collected only while it is on; turning it off stops collection and deletes unsent reports. The iCloud account and device identifiers that CloudKit adds are dropped on download and never used. Reports are not linked to your identity or used for tracking.
